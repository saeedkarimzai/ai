What this is

This is a tiny character-level neural language model. It has embeddings, a hidden neural layer, and a softmax next-character prediction layer. Training changes its weights from your text.

It is not remotely as capable as ChatGPT. That comes from model size, huge datasets, compute, and much more training. This version gives you the core idea in a Chromebook-friendly project.
